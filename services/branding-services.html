OUR DESIGN LIFECYCLE

The 4-Step Brand Identity Process.

How we build distinctive corporate identity systems from market research to full rollout.

PHASE 01

Discovery & Moodboarding

We analyze competitor identities, define your unique market positioning, and curate visual moodboards reflecting your corporate vision.

PHASE 02

Concept Ideation & Vectoring

We explore distinct geometric concepts, craft bespoke logo marks, and test typography pairings across light and dark backgrounds.

PHASE 03

Stationery & Collateral Design

We apply the chosen visual mark to corporate business cards, letterheads, social media kits, pitch decks, and digital banners.

PHASE 04

Brand Guidelines Manual

We compile your comprehensive Brand Guidelines PDF covering clear space, incorrect usage, color codes, and typography hierarchy.
